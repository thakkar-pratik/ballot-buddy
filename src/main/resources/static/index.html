How to Cast Your Vote

Empower yourself with the knowledge of the voting process.

Step 1: First Polling Officer

The first polling officer checks your name on the electoral roll and asks for your identity proof (e.g., EPIC/Voter ID card).

Step 2: Second Polling Officer

The second polling officer marks your finger with indelible ink, gives you a slip, and takes your signature on a register.

Step 3: Casting the Vote

Go to the voting compartment. Press the blue button on the Electronic Voting Machine (EVM) against the candidate of your choice.

BALLOTING UNIT
1 Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) 🪷
2 Indian National Congress (INC) ✋
3 Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) 🧹
4 Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP) 🐘

Step 4: VVPAT Verification

A slip will appear in the transparent window of the VVPAT machine for 7 seconds. It shows the serial number, name, and symbol of your chosen candidate before dropping into the sealed box.
